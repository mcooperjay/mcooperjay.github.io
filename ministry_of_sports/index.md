# ⚽ Ministry of Sports

A running series of sports analytics mini-projects — statistical models, data visualizations, and applied analysis, each paired with a short video walkthrough on LinkedIn.

The name is a nod to my time serving as a missionary in South Africa, home to the real Ministry of Sports, Arts and Culture — this is my own small, ongoing ministry of sorts: using data to better understand the games we love.

---

## Projects

::: {.grid}

::: {.g-col-6}
### [Does College Pedigree Predict NBA Career Length?](nba-blue-blood-careers.qmd)
A Bayesian (Poisson-Gamma) analysis comparing career length between "blue blood" college program alumni and other NBA players, with 95% credible intervals on the difference in means.
:::

::: {.g-col-6}
### [What Predicts an NHL Player's Plus-Minus Score?](nhl-plus-minus-model.qmd)
Multiple linear regression with cross-validation, plus a [live interactive explorer](https://nhl-explorer.streamlit.app/) built with Streamlit.
